The opportunity

Recognise excellence. Stand alongside the leaders of CSI.

In 2019, Corporate Social Responsibility News conceptualised the South African CSI Legacy Awards to recognise and celebrate best practice in the CSI industry. Valuable work is being undertaken by businesses, social entrepreneurs, NGOs, captains of industry, academics, community activists, CSI practitioners and industry enthusiasts — and it deserves to be seen and recognised.

The awards honour and showcase South African and SADC companies which have conducted outstanding work in the field of social and economic development. They uncover the principles that make for effective and impactful CSI — giving partners a platform to align their brand with credible, verified impact.
